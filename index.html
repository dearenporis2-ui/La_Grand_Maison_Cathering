About La Grande Maison

      
Food is more than
what is on the plate.

      
Chef Christelle Verheyden and her team draw on the freshest Seychelles ingredients and classic technique, treating every guest to memories made in the comfort of their own home.

    

    

      

        
      

      

        
      

    

  






  

    

      
    

    

      
Chef Christelle Verheyden

      
A creole-inspired philosophy

      
Everything we do at La Grande Maison Atelier — using subtle textures and flavours, sourcing the freshest products — has the same goal: to enhance and elevate local ingredients. Seychelles are a constant source of creativity from which we draw.

      
We combine this creative energy with classic cooking techniques and a sense of creole inspiration, so every guest discovers a new approach to the Creole taste — treated in the comfort of their own home.
